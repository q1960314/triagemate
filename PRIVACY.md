# Privacy Policy — TriageMate for Jira

Last updated: 2026-11-25.

TriageMate is a Forge app that proposes triage suggestions (priority, labels, owner) for Jira issues. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## What the app accesses

When you open an issue with the TriageMate panel, or ask the Triage Agent in Rovo chat, the app reads the fields of that issue and of related issues in the same project: summary, description, issue type, status, priority, labels, assignee, reporter, linked issues, and due date. It uses those fields only to compute the suggestion shown to you.

## What the app stores

Nothing. TriageMate keeps no database and stores no issue data outside your Atlassian site. Suggestions are computed on demand inside the Forge runtime and shown to you; they are not sent anywhere else.

## What the app writes

Only when you confirm: applying a suggestion updates that issue's priority, labels, and assignee in Jira. Nothing is written without your confirmation.

## Third parties, analytics, cookies

None. The app calls no third-party service, runs no analytics, and sets no cookies.
